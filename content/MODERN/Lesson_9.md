## Pattern Practice

*Practice these patterns out loud. The goal is automatic production.*

### Statements (7)

**1. Possessive + phone:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="nǐ">nǐ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="*de*">*de*</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="diànHuà">diànHuà</span>！
> *Your phone!*

**2. Subject + dǎ diànhuà:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wánG">wánG</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="zǒng">zǒng</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="dǎ">dǎ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="diànHuà">diànHuà</span>。
> *Boss Wang is making a phone call.*

**3. Phone greeting:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Wèi">Wèi</span>？
> *Hello? (answering phone)*

**4. hǎo + subject + verb (agreeing to action):**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="hǎo">hǎo</span>，<span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒ">wǒ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="děng">děng</span>。
> *OK, I'll wait.*

**5. Subject + wèn + object:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒ">wǒ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Wèn">Wèn</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="TĀ">TĀ</span>。
> *I'll ask him.*

**6. bùyào + verb (negative command):**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Bù">Bù</span><span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Yào">Yào</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="děng">děng</span>！
> *Don't wait!*

**7. Command + location:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="láI">láI</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="GŌNGSĪ">GŌNGSĪ</span>！
> *Come to the company!*
