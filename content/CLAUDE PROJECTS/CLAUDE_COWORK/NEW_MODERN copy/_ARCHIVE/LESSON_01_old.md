## Practice Patterns

> **💡 Pattern:** A <span style="color:#a78bfa">hěn</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>
> *"A is good" — 很 links subject to adjective*

1. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hěn</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. — I am good.

2. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hěn</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. — You are good.

3. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hěn</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. — He is good.

> **💡 Pattern:** A <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>
> *"A is not good" — 不 negates*

4. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. — I am not good.

5. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. — He is not good.

> **💡 Pattern:** A <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>
> *"A's" — 的 shows possession*

6. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>. — Mine.

7. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>. — His.
