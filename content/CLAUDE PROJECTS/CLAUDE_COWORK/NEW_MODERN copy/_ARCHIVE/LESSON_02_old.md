## Practice Patterns

> **💡 Pattern:** <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Zhè</span>/<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Nà</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéN</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*me*</span>?
> *"What is this/that?" — 什麼 asks about things*

1. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Zhè</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéN</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*me*</span>? — What is this?

2. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Nà</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéN</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*me*</span>? — What is that?

> **💡 Pattern:** A <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéI</span>?
> *"Who is A?" — 誰 asks about people*

3. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéI</span>? — Who is he?

4. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Nà</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">réN</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéI</span>? — Who is that person?

> **💡 Pattern:** A <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Jiào</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéN</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*me*</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">mínG</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*zi*</span>?
> *"What is A's name?" — 叫 means "to be called"*

5. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Jiào</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéN</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*me*</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">mínG</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*zi*</span>? — What is your name?

6. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Jiào</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">shéN</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*me*</span>? — What is he called?

> **💡 Pattern:** A <span style="color:#a78bfa">yǒu</span> / A <span style="color:#6edcb8">méI</span><span style="color:#a78bfa">yǒu</span>
> *"A has / A doesn't have" — 沒 negates 有 (not 不)*

7. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">yǒu</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">mínG</span><span style="color:#ffd966">*zi*</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span>? — Does he have a name?
