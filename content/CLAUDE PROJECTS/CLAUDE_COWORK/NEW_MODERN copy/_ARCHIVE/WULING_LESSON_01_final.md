## Vocab 1


> [!warning] New Vocab
> 
> - <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> = I, me (我)
>  <br>
> - <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> = you (你)
> - <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> = he, him (他)

### Statments

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>. = I. / Me.

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>. = You.

<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span>. = He/She - Him/Her 

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>? <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>? <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span>? = Me? You? Him/Her?

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>, <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>, <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span>. = You, me, him/her.
