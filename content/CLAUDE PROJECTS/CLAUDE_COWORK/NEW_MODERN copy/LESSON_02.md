## 🟨 New Words (1-3)

> **這** zhè — this
> **那** nà — that
> **什麼** shénme — what

---

## 📝 Statements

1. 這是什麼？ | zhè shì shénme? | What is this?
2. 那是什麼？ | nà shì shénme? | What is that?
3. 這很好。 | zhè hěn hǎo. | This is very good.
4. 那不好。 | nà bù hǎo. | That's not good.

---

## 🟨 New Words (4-6)

> **誰** shéi — who
> **人** rén — person
> **有** yǒu — have, there is

---

## 📝 Statements

5. 他是誰？ | tā shì shéi? | Who is he?
6. 那人是誰？ | nà rén shì shéi? | Who is that person?
7. 這是誰的？ | zhè shì shéi de? | Whose is this?
8. 我有。 | wǒ yǒu. | I have (it).
9. 他有嗎？ | tā yǒu ma? | Does he have (it)?

---

## 🟨 New Words (7-9)

> **沒** méi — not have
> **叫** jiào — to be called
> **名字** míngzi — name

---

## 📝 Statements

10. 我沒有。 | wǒ méi yǒu. | I don't have (it).
11. 他沒有名字。 | tā méi yǒu míngzi. | He doesn't have a name.
12. 你叫什麼名字？ | nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? | What's your name?
13. 他叫什麼？ | tā jiào shénme? | What is he called?
14. 那人叫什麼名字？ | nà rén jiào shénme míngzi? | What's that person's name?

---

## 📖 Full Story

*Questions without answers. Someone is watching.*

1. 那是什麼？ | nà shì shénme? | What is that?
2. 那是人嗎？ | nà shì rén ma? | Is that a person?
3. 他是誰？ | tā shì shéi? | Who is he?
4. 他有名字嗎？ | tā yǒu míngzi ma? | Does he have a name?
5. 他叫什麼？ | tā jiào shénme? | What is he called?
6. 他沒有名字。 | tā méi yǒu míngzi. | He doesn't have a name.
7. 這是誰的？ | zhè shì shéi de? | Whose is this?
8. 是他的。 | shì tā de. | It's his.
9. 他不好。 | tā bù hǎo. | He is not good.
10. 那人是誰？ | nà rén shì shéi? | Who is that person?
