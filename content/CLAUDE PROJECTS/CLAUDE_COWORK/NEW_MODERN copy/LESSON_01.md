> **是** shì — is, am, are
> **他** tā — he, him

---

## 📖 Full Story

*A simple exchange.*

1. 你好！ | nǐ hǎo! | Hello!
2. 你好！你好嗎？ | nǐ hǎo! nǐ hǎo ma? | Hello! How are you?
3. 我很好。你好嗎？ | wǒ hěn hǎo. nǐ hǎo ma? | I'm very good. How are you?
4. 我不好。 | wǒ bù hǎo. | I'm not good.
5. 他好嗎？ | tā hǎo ma? | Is he good?
6. 他？他很好。 | tā? tā hěn hǎo. | Him? He's very good.
7. 是你的嗎？ | shì nǐ de ma? | Is it yours?
8. 不是我的。 | bù shì wǒ de. | It's not mine.
9. 是他的嗎？ | shì tā de ma? | Is it his?
10. 是。是他的。 | shì. shì tā de. | Yes. It's his.
