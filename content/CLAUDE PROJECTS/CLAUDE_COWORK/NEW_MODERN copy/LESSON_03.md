## 🟨 New Words (1-3)

> **在** zài — at, in
> **裡** lǐ — inside
> **上** shàng — on, above

---

## 📝 Statements

1. 他在這裡。 | tā zài zhèlǐ. | He is here.
2. 人在裡嗎？ | rén zài lǐ ma? | Is someone inside?
3. 在上。 | zài shàng. | It's on top.
4. 他在那裡。 | tā zài nàlǐ. | He is there.

---

## 🟨 New Words (4-6)

> **去** qù — to go
> **來** lái — to come
> **走** zǒu — to walk

---

## 📝 Statements

5. 我去。 | wǒ qù. | I'll go.
6. 他來嗎？ | tā lái ma? | Is he coming?
7. 你走嗎？ | nǐ zǒu ma? | Are you leaving?
8. 來！ | lái! | Come!
9. 他不去。 | tā bù qù. | He's not going.

---

## 🟨 New Words (7-9)

> **看** kàn — to look, see
> **哪** nǎ — where, which
> **這裡** zhèlǐ — here

---

## 📝 Statements

10. 看！ | kàn! | Look!
11. 他在哪？ | tā zài nǎ? | Where is he?
12. 這裡很好。 | zhèlǐ hěn hǎo. | It's very good here.
13. 你去哪？ | nǐ qù nǎ? | Where are you going?
14. 來這裡！ | lái zhèlǐ! | Come here!

---

## 📖 Full Story

*Movement in the unknown. Someone is looking for someone.*

1. 他在哪？ | tā zài nǎ? | Where is he?
2. 他不在這裡。 | tā bù zài zhèlǐ. | He's not here.
3. 那人在那裡嗎？ | nà rén zài nàlǐ ma? | Is that person over there?
4. 看！ | kàn! | Look!
5. 他來嗎？ | tā lái ma? | Is he coming?
6. 他不來。 | tā bù lái. | He's not coming.
7. 我去。 | wǒ qù. | I'll go.
8. 你去哪？ | nǐ qù nǎ? | Where are you going?
9. 我走。 | wǒ zǒu. | I'm leaving.
10. 這裡不好。 | zhèlǐ bù hǎo. | It's not good here.
