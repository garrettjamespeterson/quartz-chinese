## Vocab

> [!info] New Words
>
> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> = **good**
> <br>
> <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span> = **(yes/no question particle)**

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>! = Hello!

This is the most common greeting in Mandarin. Literally: "you good!"


> **Tone Sandhi:** <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> is 3rd tone. <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> is 3rd tone. When two 3rd tones appear in a row, the first one is spoken as a 2nd tone (rising). So <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span><span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> is *pronounced* "níhǎo" — but the characters and dictionary forms don't change.

Adding <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span> to any statement turns it into a yes/no question:

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span>? = How are you? *(Literally: "you good?")*

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. = I'm good.

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. = I'm not good.

> **Pop-Up Grammar:** <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span> turns ANY statement into a yes/no question. Just stick it on the end. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> = you're good. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span>? = are you good?

> [!info] New Words
>
> <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> = **he, she, him, her**
> <br>
> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> = **(possessive — like 's)**

<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> works for both "he" and "she" — spoken Mandarin uses the same word.

<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>... = He/She is...

<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>. = He/She is not me.

Now add <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> after any pronoun to make it possessive:

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> = my / mine

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> = your / yours

<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> = his / hers

> **Pop-Up Grammar:** <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> works like ('s) in English. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> = my. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> = your. Simple.

## Gameplay: The Voice

*The darkness from before has thinned. You can sense a shape — the same shadow that spoke to you. But now it steps forward. Not a shadow anymore. A figure.*

*It speaks:*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>."

*A greeting. The first human thing you've heard.*

**→ Greet them back:**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>.

</details>

---

*The figure tilts its head.*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span>?"
> = How are you?

*You think. Are you good? You woke up in total darkness with no memory. Are you good?*

**→ How do you answer? Pick one:**

A) <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. = I'm good.

B) <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. = I'm not good.

*Both are correct. This is your story.*

---

*The figure takes another step closer. You can see it now — barely. Tall. Dressed in something dark.*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>... <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>."
> = I'm... I'm not good.

*It gestures behind itself, into the deeper darkness. Something is there.*

> "<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span>."

*One word. Pointing into the dark. Someone else is here.*

**→ The figure just said "TĀ" — what does that mean?**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> = he / she / him / her. There's a third person here.

</details>

---

*You peer into the darkness. You can't see anyone else. The figure shakes its head.*

> "<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span>... <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>."
> = He/She is not good. He/She... is not good.

**→ Is this third person doing well?**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. Not good.

</details>

---

*The figure reaches toward you. In its hand — something small. You can't see what it is.*

> "<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span>?"
> = Is this yours?

**→ Is it yours? Answer yes or no:**

A) <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>. = Yes. It's mine.

B) <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>. = No. It's not mine.

---

*The figure looks down at the object, then back at you.*

> "<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>."
> = It's not mine. It's not yours.

*A pause.*

> "<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>."
> = It's his/hers.

*The figure places the object on the ground between you and turns toward the deeper darkness.*

**→ Whose is the object?**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>. = His/Hers. It belongs to the third person — the one in the darkness.

</details>

---

*The figure stops. Without turning around:*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span> <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span>?"

*It walks into the dark. You are alone again — but not quite. The object is still there. And somewhere ahead, there are two others.*

### Key Patterns

- **<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">hǎo</span>** = Hello (literally "you good")
- **statement + <span style="color:#ffd966">*ma*</span>?** = yes/no question
- **pronoun + <span style="color:#ffd966">*de*</span>** = possessive (my, your, his/hers)

### Tone Sandhi Notes

**你好** (nǐhǎo): Two 3rd tones in a row → first becomes 2nd tone. Pronounced "níhǎo." You don't need to think about this — your ear will learn it naturally from the audio.

**不好** (bù hǎo): 不 is 4th tone, 好 is 3rd tone. No change here — sandhi only applies when 不 is before another 4th tone (like 不是 → búshì).
