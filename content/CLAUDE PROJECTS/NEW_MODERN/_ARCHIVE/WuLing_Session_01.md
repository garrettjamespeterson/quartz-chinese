## Mini-Lesson

> [!info] New Words
>
> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> = **I, me**
> <br>
> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> = **you**

Point to yourself. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>.

Point to someone else. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>.

When you talk about yourself, you always say <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> — no matter who you are.
When you talk to someone, you call them <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>.

### Drill

**1.** If I am talking about myself, I say: ___

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>

</details>

**2.** If I am talking to you, I say: ___

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>

</details>

**3.** I say "<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>." Now YOU say it — when you talk about yourself, you also say: ___

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> — because now you're talking about yourself!

</details>

**4.** If someone calls you <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>, and you want to refer back to them, you say: ___

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>

</details>

**5.** <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>, <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>, <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>, <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> — say them back and forth. Point to yourself, then away. Yourself. Away.

**10.** <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>? — This means "is or isn't?" A way to ask "right?" in Mandarin. Say it: <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>?

> **Pop-Up Grammar:** This is the **A-not-A** question pattern. Instead of adding a question word, you say the verb, then "not," then the verb again. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>? = "Is or isn't?"

**11.** <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>, <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>? = "I am me, right?" → ___

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>! = Yes!

</details>

**12.** <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>, <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>? = "You are me, right?" → ___

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>! = No! (You are not me.)

</details>

## Gameplay: Awakening

*Darkness. Complete darkness. No ground, no sky, no edges. Then — a voice, somewhere close.*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>... <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> who?"

*You can't see who's speaking. You try to think. Who are you? You don't remember — not your name, not your face. But you feel yourself breathing. You exist.*

**→ Answer the voice. Say: "<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>..."**

*You don't have an answer yet. But the words come anyway.*

---

*The voice presses closer:*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>, <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>?"
> = Are you me, or not?

**→ Are you the voice? Answer:**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>. = No. I am not you.

</details>

---

*The voice pauses. When it speaks again, it's slower — almost relieved.*

> "<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>."
> = Yes. You are not me. I am not you.

*A shape begins to form in the darkness — not a person yet, more like a shadow remembering how to be one.*

> "But <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>... here. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>... also here."

**→ The voice says "you are here." Now say "I am here," using what you know:**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>... here. (Chinglish — "here" hasn't been taught yet, and that's fine.)

</details>

---

*The shadow speaks faster now, testing you:*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>."
> = I am me.

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>."
> = You are you.

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>."
> = I am not you.

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>."
> = You are not me.

**→ Can you say all four lines back? Try it out loud before revealing the answer.**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>. = I am me.

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>. = You are you.

<span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>. = I am not you.

<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>. = You are not me.

</details>

---

*The darkness thins one shade. You can almost see the shape now — a silhouette, facing you. It leans closer.*

> "<span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>?"

*It sounds like a question — but also like a test.*

**→ Confirm who you are:**

<details><summary>→</summary>

<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>. = Yes. I am me.

</details>

---

*The shadow nods — or something like a nod. The darkness loosens.*

> "Remember. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>. <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> something else. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>."

*The voice fades. The darkness remains, but thinner now. You are here. You are you. That's all you know — and for now, it's enough.*

### Words Learned This Session

|--------|-----------|---------|
| <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> | <span style="color:#a78bfa">我</span> | I, me |

### Key Patterns

- **A <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> B** = A is B
- **A <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> B** = A is not B
- **<span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Bù</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span>?** = Is it or isn't it? (A-not-A question)

### Tone Sandhi Note

不 (bù, 4th tone) changes to bú (2nd tone) before another 4th tone. So 不是 is pronounced "búshì" even though both characters are 4th tone. You'll hear this naturally — don't worry about memorizing the rule yet.
