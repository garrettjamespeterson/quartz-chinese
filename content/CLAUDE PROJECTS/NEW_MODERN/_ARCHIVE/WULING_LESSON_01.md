- <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span> = you (你)

## Part 1 Statements

1. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>. = I. / Me.

2. <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>. = You.

3. <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span>. = He. / Him.

4. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span>? <span style="color:#a78bfa">nǐ</span>? <span style="color:#ff6b35">TĀ</span>? = Me? You? Him?
