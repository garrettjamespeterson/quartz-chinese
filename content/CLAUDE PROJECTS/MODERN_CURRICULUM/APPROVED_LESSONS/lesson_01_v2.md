## Pattern Practice

### Statements

1. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Shì</span> <span style="color:#6edcb8">réN</span>. = I am a person.

4. <span style="color:#a78bfa">wǒ</span> <span style="color:#b0b0b0">Jiào</span> Kevin. = I am called Kevin.

### Question-Answer Pairs

Question 8.
Q: nǐ Shì XĪNréN ma? = Are you a new person?
A: Shì, wǒ Shì XĪNréN. = Yes, I am a new person.

Question 9.
Q: nǐ Jiào shéNme mínGzi? = What is your name?
A: wǒ Jiào lǐLì. = I am called Li Li.

Question 10.
Q: TĀ Shì shéI? = Who is he?
A: TĀ Shì wánG zǒng. = He is Boss Wang.
