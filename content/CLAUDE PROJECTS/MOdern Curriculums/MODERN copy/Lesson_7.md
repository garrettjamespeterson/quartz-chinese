## Pattern Practice

*Practice these patterns out loud. The goal is automatic production.*

### Statements (7)

**1. Time of day + action:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="ZǍOSHANG">ZǍOSHANG</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒ">wǒ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="qǐCHUÁNG">qǐCHUÁNG</span>。
> *In the morning, I get up.*

**2. Subject + time + action:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒ">wǒ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="ZǍOSHANG">ZǍOSHANG</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Liù">Liù</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="diǎn">diǎn</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="qǐCHUÁNG">qǐCHUÁNG</span>。
> *I get up at 6 o'clock in the morning.*

**3. Now + time:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="xiànZÀI">xiànZÀI</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="BĀ">BĀ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="diǎn">diǎn</span>。
> *It's 8 o'clock now.*

**4. Subject + hěn + adjective:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="nǐ">nǐ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="hěn">hěn</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǎn">wǎn</span>。
> *You're (very) late.*

**5. Subject + hěn + adjective + action:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒ">wǒ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="hěn">hěn</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="zǎo">zǎo</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="SHUÌJiào">SHUÌJiào</span>。
> *I sleep (very) early.*

**6. bùyào + action (negative command):**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Bù">Bù</span><span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Yào">Yào</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="SHUÌJiào">SHUÌJiào</span>！
> *Don't sleep!*

**7. yào + adjective + action (should):**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Yào">Yào</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="zǎo">zǎo</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="SHUÌJiào">SHUÌJiào</span>！
> *Sleep early! / You should sleep early!*
