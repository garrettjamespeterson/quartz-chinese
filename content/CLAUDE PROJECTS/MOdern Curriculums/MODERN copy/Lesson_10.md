## Pattern Practice

*Practice these patterns out loud. The goal is automatic production.*

### Statements (7)

**1. Day of the week:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="XĪNGQĪWǓ">XĪNGQĪWǓ</span>！
> *Friday!*

**2. Subject + xiǎng + verb + object:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒ">wǒ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="xiǎng">xiǎng</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Kàn">Kàn</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="diànYǐng">diànYǐng</span>。
> *I want to watch a movie.*

**3. Subject + xiǎng + verb:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒ">wǒ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="xiǎng">xiǎng</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="XIŪXI">XIŪXI</span>。
> *I want to rest.*

**4. Subject + yào + verb:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="nǐ">nǐ</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Yào">Yào</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="XIŪXI">XIŪXI</span>！
> *You need to rest!*

**5. qù + verb (go do something):**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Qù">Qù</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wán">wán</span>！
> *Go play!*

**6. Subject + qù + location:**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wǒmen">wǒmen</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="Qù">Qù</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="nàr">nàr</span>。
> *We go there.*

**7. Echo question (surprised/confirming):**
> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wán">wán</span>？<span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="shénme">shénme</span> <span class="zhongwen-pinyin" data-pinyin="wán">wán</span>？
> *Play? What play?*
