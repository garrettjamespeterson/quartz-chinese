## Introduction

This culminating lesson presents the complete original passage from the Huangdi Neijing (黃帝內經), Suwen Chapter 5. After completing Lessons 1-7, students should now be able to read and understand this classical medical text on yin-yang theory as it applies to flavor, qi, form, and essence.

## Section 2: Plain Character Reader

```zh-cn
水為陰，火為陽，陽為氣，陰為味。

味歸形，形歸氣，氣歸精，精歸化。

精食氣，形食味，化生精，氣生形。

味傷形，氣傷精，精化為氣，氣傷於味。

陰味出下竅，陽氣出上竅。

味厚者為陰，薄為陰之陽。

氣厚者為陽，薄為陽之陰。

味厚則泄，薄則通。

氣薄則發泄，厚則發熱。

壯火之氣衰，少火之氣壯。

壯火食氣，氣食少火。

壯火散氣，少火生氣。

氣味，辛甘發散為陽，酸苦涌泄為陰。
```

## Summary of Key Concepts

### Yin-Yang Correspondences
| Yin (陰) | Yang (陽) |
|----------|-----------|
| Water (水) | Fire (火) |
| Flavor (味) | Qi (氣) |
| Lower (下) | Upper (上) |
| Thick flavor | Thin flavor |
| Thin qi | Thick qi |
| Sour, Bitter (酸苦) | Acrid, Sweet (辛甘) |
| Surge, Discharge (涌泄) | Emit, Disperse (發散) |

### The Cycle of Returns
味 → 形 → 氣 → 精 → 化

### Fire Dynamics
- **Strong Fire (壯火)**: Consumes and disperses qi; its own qi declines
- **Weak Fire (少火)**: Generates qi; its qi becomes strong
