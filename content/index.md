# 學中文 Learn Chinese

A curriculum for clinicians and students learning Chinese language — focused on reading medical texts and classical sources.

## How to Use This Site

Start with the basics and work through in order, or search for specific characters and grammar patterns. The graph shows how lessons connect. Drop questions in the comments under any page.

## The Curriculum

- **Characters** — radicals, components, medical vocabulary
- **Grammar** — classical and modern patterns
- **Reading Practice** — excerpts from Nèijīng, Shānghánlùn, and clinical texts
